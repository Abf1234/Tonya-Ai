# User guide

## Verify Before You Share

Start on the home page and enter a suspicious message, claim or URL. You can also select a screenshot or document in the foundation interface. The current phase carries the selection into the verification workspace but does not upload it or call an AI provider.

## Truth Guardian chat

The Truth Guardian route provides the future conversational interface. The current foundation mode accepts local text only and clearly reports that no model was called. A later version will answer from scoped trusted evidence and show citations.

## Report suspicious activity

The reporting hub is visible as a public route, but report submission is intentionally disabled until the PostgreSQL-backed fraud report model, privacy controls, upload scanning and Google Sheets retry workflow are implemented.

## Trusted information

Alerts, fact checks, verified information, trusted sources and the learning centre are structured foundation pages. No fake incidents, fake official sources or unverified public claims are displayed.

## Safety and privacy

Do not submit passwords, one-time codes, payment PINs or unnecessary identity documents. When reporting a real concern later, provide only the information needed for verification and review. Public duplicate results must never reveal private reporter details.

## Lite Mode

The Lite Mode control reduces motion and visual transitions for a lighter, text-first browsing experience. It is a browser preference and does not change the evidence or status of a future result.
